### Post Text:

**Tired of resumes? Here's what I built instead.**

For years, resumes have been static PDFs — a one-size-fits-all format that tells you *what* someone did, but not *why*, *how*, or *how well* they did it.

I decided to flip the script.

**Enter: John's Career Copilot** — a full-stack AI application that lets visitors have a real conversation about my background.

**The Tech Stack:**
- 🧠 **Dual LLM Architecture:** Google Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite (speed) + DeepSeek R1 (reasoning)
- ⚡ **Optimized Frontend:** React 18 + Vite (~50 kB gzipped)
- 🔧 **Express.js Backend** with structured JSON resume integration
- 🐳 **Production Deployment:** Docker, Caddy HTTPS, VPS infrastructure
- 🎨 **Mobile-First Design:** Dark/light themes, responsive UI

**The Innovation:** Instead of traditional keyword matching, visitors ask natural questions:
- "What's your experience with infrastructure?" 
- "Tell me about your AI work"
- "What cost savings have you achieved?"

The AI *understands context* and pulls from a structured career profile, generating thoughtful, cited responses in real-time.

**The Result:** A portfolio that doesn't just list achievements — it *explains* them through intelligent conversation.

This is beyond "AI resume builder." This is **interactive career storytelling powered by LLMs.**

**See it live:** www.askcareer-ai.com

Want to build something like this? The architecture is surprisingly elegant:
1. Structure career data as JSON
2. Feed it to multiple LLM endpoints via OpenRouter
3. Let natural language do the heavy lifting
4. Deploy containerized with auto-HTTPS
